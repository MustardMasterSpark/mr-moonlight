Hello, and welcome to the Mr. Moonlight demo for ELVTR!
This prototype mainly focuses on gunplay. Many things will change...
Right now, it is a faster gameplay than the intended one for the single player mode.
Use number keys 1-5 to switch weapon classes (7 for grenades).
Press the same weapon key again to cycle through weapons in that class.
The developer is kind of a lazy bum, so the frag grenade doesn’t work yet.
Press Esc to pause and view the key bindings. Check out the cheat modes!
Press Alt+F4 to quit. Yes, the developer hasn’t implemented a decent quit option yet.
A complete demo and a Kickstarter are in the works. More updates are coming. Please support us!
