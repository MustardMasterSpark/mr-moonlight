# Mr. Moonlight — Character Profiles, extracted

Source: `MrMoonlight Character Profiles.pdf`, Mustard 2026.
Eight characters. Used for **voice direction**, **dialogue writing**, **character modelling and rigging**, and **animation intent**.

> **Handling note.** The "Contextual Spoilers" sections contain heavy material — sexual violence, abortion, suicide, self-harm, eating disorders, child abuse. This is fictional backstory for a Mature/AO-rated game and it is load-bearing: it is *why* each character speaks the way they do. It is **subtext, not text** — almost none of it is spoken aloud in the demo. Use it to inform performance and writing. Do not put it on screen.

**Demo speaking parts:** Tracey, Rylee (radio only), Holly, Vernon, Scott, Shannon (one line, radio). **Robert and William do not speak in the demo** — they appear only in the Polaroid and in Rylee's monologue.

## TRACEY — the player

**Theresa Gallagher** · Jung archetype: **The Hero** · Age 25 (b. 1953)
Irish American, born in Ireland. Accent: **mostly American Northwest; when she talks fast, some Irish slips through.**
5'6" / 168 cm · 65 kg

**Physical.** Slim, not skinny — though she always wears baggy clothes. **Redhead**, green eyes, freckles. Ear piercings on both ears. **Always grumpy or with an angry face.** Never uses makeup.

**Who she is.** Born into a traditional Irish family that moved to the USA when she was little. As a kid she was fascinated by museums and wanted to study history. At 25 she is a **college dropout** with bad habits and a grumpy attitude. Works in a library, lives in a cheap small apartment she shares with **Rylee**. Doesn't like to talk much, but keeps in touch — mainly with Holly and Rylee.

**Likes:** history, punk culture, drugs and alcohol.
**Dislikes:** crowds, dogs, coffee.
**Nicknames:** Trey · Punk *(Rylee)* · Princess *(Rylee)* · Potato Digger *(William, historical banter)*

**Sample lines (tone reference):**
- *"Yes… this is fun…"* — asked how she's feeling on the camping trip
- *"What the fuck did you even call me for?"* — thanking Rylee for a birthday invitation
- *"If you really are my sister, then pass me that bottle"* — showing Holly some gratitude

**Inspirations:** the grumpy **Vivian James**; the survivor **Ellen Ripley**.

**Wardrobe (steal her drip).** Black beanie · black leather punk jump boots · leather utility belt · **German flecktarn parka** (no German flag) · big black T-shirt underneath · black baggy cargo pants · long colourful argyle socks *(she doesn't like the colours; they were the only clean pair)* · thick black huggie and snug earrings, both ears.

> **Modelling notes.** The beanie and the boots are the silhouette. The **argyle socks matter** — they are what you see in Scenes 1–6 when the boots are missing, so the barefoot/sock model swap is a visible gag, not just a stat change. The parka is bulky; the arms are what the player sees constantly in first person.

**Subtext (do not state on screen).** Years before the game she was gang-raped and the assault left her pregnant. She developed serious trauma and PTSD; the bitterness is a defense, and when she is vulnerable she can break down or fall into panic attacks. Her addictions grew out of that trauma — a way to stay disconnected. After an incident with Holly she had an abortion; she had made peace with the pregnancy and wanted to keep the baby, and losing it damaged her further. She carries heavy guilt that has damaged most of her family relationships and friendships. **Above all, she regrets what happened with Scott.**

> **Why this matters for the demo.** It explains why she is warmest with Scott and hardest on herself; why "Oh God..." after the first kill is refusal, not devotion; why she recognises Vernon's morphine instantly and says nothing; and why Holly using "Trey" lands the way it does.

## HOLLY

**Holly Shepherd** · Jung archetype: **The Innocent** · Age 24 (b. 1954)
American. Accent: American Northwest.
5'5" / 165 cm · 63 kg

**Physical.** Slim and athletic — she was the popular girl. Dresses very femininely. **Black hair, blue eyes**, fair skin with **a single freckle on her left cheek**. Loves to genuinely smile and usually seems happy. A little makeup, just to highlight her pretty features.

**Who she is.** Tracey's and Holly's fathers met at work right after Tracey's family moved to Alaska; since they were little, **Holly has been like a sister to Tracey**. Very kind and happy, a hippie who loves nature. Now a **kindergarten teacher**. Her mother divorced her father, and her father died right before she graduated; she lives alone in the inherited house. She is confused and a bit annoyed by Tracey's distance, and doesn't understand why Tracey turned down her offer to move in.

**Likes:** nature, children, knitting.
**Dislikes:** violence, sad people, rules.
**Nicknames:** Miss Perfect *(Tracey)*

**Sample lines:**
- *"Ah, don't you worry! Tomorrow will be a new day!"* — cheering up Shannon
- *"Can I help you with something?"* — showing up uninvited to Tracey's apartment
- *"Oh! That's pretty"* — snatching a flower away from William

**Inspirations:** the always-positive **Mavis**; the psychotic **Charles Manson**.

**Wardrobe.** Long retro hippie boots · pantyhose · **golden ankh necklace** · long wine-coloured skirt · custom-made turtleneck sweaters (black background with bright patterns and shapes).

**Subtext — MEGA SPOILERS (do not state on screen).** **She is a witch.** She is responsible for Tracey's abortion. She enables Tracey's addictions. She is a bastard child *(Vernon is her real father)*. She made her mother divorce her father and then killed her father. **She planned the trip so the cult could sacrifice her friends.** She is good at lying. She is a very jealous and unhappy person.

> **Why this matters for Scene 8.** Every warm line Holly gives in the cabin is a lie she half-believes. The two inspirations are the direction: play **Mavis on the surface, Manson underneath**. Vernon's *"WHAT HAVE YOU DONE?!"* is the only honest reading of that room, and the player is not meant to understand it yet. The **ankh** is the tell — it is the one non-Christian religious object in a scene full of crosses.

## RYLEE

**Rylee Doe** · Jung archetype: **The Caregiver** · Age 24 (b. 1955)
American. Accent: American Northwest.
5'7" / 170 cm · 83 kg

**Physical.** **Chubby and very lively.** Blonde. Hazel eyes, freckles on her cheeks. Energetic and happy. Likes **heavy but beautiful makeup, sometimes in electric colours** — a pioneer for the 70s.

**Who she is.** An orphan raised in a Catholic orphanage. Kindhearted but **slow on the uptake**, and she often misses the mood of a room. Despite being raised by nuns she has **a mouth like a pirate** that can make even Tracey and Robert uncomfortable. Can be kind of racist among friends. Works part-time as a waitress, currently lives with Tracey, has started training to become a nurse — despite Tracey's recommendation that she become a circus clown. **Loves playing her guitar.**

**Likes:** music, ice cream, jokes, bad jokes, very very bad jokes.
**Dislikes:** boring people, nuns, California.
**Nicknames:** Lady Jester *(Tracey)* · "Miss who ate my fucking sandwich again?" *(Tracey)* · Sunrise *(Robert)*

**Sample lines:**
- *"What do you call it when an orphan gets kidnapped?... ehh? ehhh?.... a surprise adoption..."* — interrupting Scott's telescope explanation
- *"You know? I could be a very good lesbian, I'm good with my guitar"* — asking Tracey for rent money
- *"I'm sorry Chief, but the white woman wants a banana split"* — asking Robert to hang out

**Inspirations:** the late **Robin Williams**; the potty-mouthed **Kenny and Cartman**; the golden-hearted **Big Bird**.

**Wardrobe.** Lots of smiley pins on her guitar strap *(and a smiley sticker with 'X X' eyes on the back of the guitar)* · leather greatcoat · yellow turtleneck sweater · **green scapular underneath** · yellow Converse with Sharpie doodles (smiley faces, flowers, musical notes, hearts) · blue jeans · big gold hoop earrings.

**Subtext (do not state on screen).** She has an eating disorder. Deep insecurity and self-esteem issues that have been building since she understood she was an orphan. Despite the energetic attitude, she is usually depressed behind the closed door of her room. **Despite all the vulgar jokes and innuendo, she is a virgin.**

> **Why this matters for Scene 3.** The Robin Williams note is the key: the entire radio conversation is a performance held up over exhaustion and sadness. The filthy boasting in D-03-020 is almost certainly invented. Play the **speed and delight, never leering.** Her last line — *"We'll wait for you, barefoot princess..."* — is the last thing Tracey ever hears from her, and the broken guitar at the Glade is the payoff. The **green scapular** under a leather greatcoat is her whole character in one object.

## SCOTT

**Scott Clarke** · Jung archetype: **The Lover** · Age 26 (b. 1952)
American. Accent: American Northwest.
6'0" / 183 cm · 85 kg

**Physical.** Average build for the 70s. Brown hair, brown eyes, **bags under them**. Longish hair and unkempt facial hair. Expression usually easygoing, **a bit on the melancholic side**. Practical clothing for travelling and walking.

**Who he is.** A senior in high school when he met Tracey; **they dated.** Where she is obsessed with history, he loves **astronomy**. His biggest dream was to be an astronaut. When his Vietnam draft number approached he **chickened out and went to Canada**. He came back last year after the pardon for draft dodgers, and is still readjusting and reconnecting. Works at the same sawmill as Robert. **Ironically, he now wants to join the Air Force**, hoping to work his way up to NASA pilot.

**Likes:** flying, astronomy, **Tracey**.
**Dislikes:** the past, loneliness, death.
**Nicknames:** Rocketman *(Tracey)* · Scotsman *(Rylee)* · Drifter

**Sample lines:**
- *"You didn't think I was gone forever, did you?"* — meeting Tracey again after years
- *"The stars, heck, even the moon. They are so far away, yet we are still able to see them just by looking up"* — getting emotional about his hobby
- *"You know? I hate moving from place to place"* — telling William he doesn't share his love of long walks

**Inspirations:** the drifter **John Rambo**.

**Wardrobe.** **Wooden Latin cross necklace** · worn blue jeans · blue plaid shirt · travelling olive jacket · white tank top · worn work boots.

**Subtext (do not state on screen).** Middle brother of three. **His older brother died in Vietnam in the first draft. Then, while Scott was in Canada, his younger brother enlisted and also died in Vietnam.** The cross necklace was his younger brother's, given to him posthumously **by Tracey**. He considers himself a coward and cannot forgive himself for running. In Canada he developed a drinking problem that eased on returning. **While he was in Canada he cut ties with everyone — and it was during that time that Tracey was raped and had the abortion. He cannot forgive himself for this.** Has attempted suicide.

> **Why this matters for Scene 9.** *"I'm sorry Tracey... I'm sorry for all....."* is the load-bearing line of his entire character, and he trails off before naming what it is for. The **North Star sequence** in Scene 10 is the last time he is happy — he is dying and teaching her constellations. The **wooden cross** he wears is his dead brother's, given to him by the woman now dragging him uphill.

## SHANNON

**Shannon Baker** (Baker-Park) · Jung archetype: **The Creator** · Age 22 (b. 1957)
American, **Korean on her mother's side**. Accent: American Northwest. **She stutters a lot when nervous.**
5'9" / 175 cm · 65 kg

**Physical.** Tall and skinny, a bit underweight. Black eyes, black hair, brushed straight and flat. **Very calm and reserved; she rarely stands out in a group.** Rarely uses makeup. **Thick 70s glasses.** Freckles.

**Who she is.** Her father served in the Korean War and married a Korean woman; **beyond her looks she has no Korean cultural inheritance — her father forbade her mother to teach her the language and traditions.** She has liked to draw since she was little, but despite dreams of being an artist her parents push her toward law, like her father. **Youngest of the group.** Met Rylee and William at a hippie party a few years ago, and is grateful to have friends who don't tell her who to be.

**Likes:** justice, observing, autumn.
**Dislikes:** lawyers, yelling, control freaks.
**Nicknames:** Shinny *(William)* · Dragon Lady *(Rylee)*

**Sample lines:**
- *"P-please! D-Don't move… I'm almost d-done"* — asking Rylee to stop moving while sketching her
- *"Do you r-really think Alaska has b-better landscapes than Britain?"* — curious about William's travels
- *"You want to tag along? W-William found where the punks hang out, h-he says the music is o-ok"* — trying to pull Tracey out of her cave

**Inspirations:** the rigid **Kanamori** and the creative **Midori** from *Eizouken*; the candid **Gangle**.

**Wardrobe.** Purple-striped turtleneck sweater · long orange-brown pants with black buttons · black thermal knee socks · leather belt · pretty leather shoes · **thick 70s eyeglasses** · **leather portfolio** for her drawing supplies.

**Subtext (do not state on screen).** She indulges in psychedelics, especially LSD and shrooms. She self-harms. Her strict parents made her resent mediocrity, and at the same time she feels stagnant; her unfulfilled passions burn her inside. **There is a lot repressed inside her — to everyone's surprise, she can be extremely violent.** She admires William for his carefree nature and his freedom. She never says it out loud, but she considers herself an anarchist, and thinks the government of the USA should be punished for its long list of invasions — an idea that grew in silence thanks to her patriotic father.

> **Demo note.** Shannon has exactly **one line** (D-08-037, screamed through the radio) and then vanishes. Scott says she escaped. **She is never found in the demo.** The stutter should be present even in that scream.

## ROBERT

**Robert Pitsiulak** · Jung archetype: **The Rebel** · Age 29 (b. 1949)
American — **Native American, Inuit**. Accent: Inuit influence; **he talks slowly and heavily, sometimes mixing in Inuit words.**
6'3" / 191 cm · 99 kg

**Physical.** **A tower.** His lifestyle and lumberjack work have naturally built a solid body. Stern, unkempt buzz cut. No facial hair but **big eyebrows**. Doesn't care about fashion — almost always in work fatigues. His face is not very friendly, **but he really is not a bad person.** Deep black eyes, slightly brown skin.

**Who he is.** His Inuit father died of cirrhosis when Robert was young; after that Robert took care of his mother and sister. **Called up in the first Vietnam draft.** He joined proudly, as his people's warrior traditions demanded, but ended up as a cook — he saw little action, but he saw the destruction. Grateful to have come back alive. **In his heart he is a pacifist**, but his intimidating presence has often led to rejection, and as a defense he is often aggressive and prone to violence. He believes America is not a just country. Happy to be busy working and providing. Met Scott at work. He likes being around the group but is not good at letting his guard down. **Rylee is annoying and puzzling to him.**

**Likes:** power tools, stews and Campbell's soup, instrumental music.
**Dislikes:** corruption, alcohol, cowards.
**Nicknames:** Chief *(Rylee)* · redskin *(Rylee, teasing)*

**Sample lines:**
- *"Sometimes, a bright sun isn't enough to warm the earth."* — a strange remark thrown into a political discussion with William and Shannon
- *"This soup is terrible."* — thanking Rylee for her cooking
- *"God created the trees and the rocks. And we created boats and rifles from the trees and the rocks."* — another remark out of the blue, at a punk gathering

**Inspirations:** the stoic **Billy Sole** from *Predator*; the calculating **Golgo**; the unstoppable **Zenigata**.

**Wardrobe.** Red plaid lumberjack jacket · dark work overalls with suspenders · heavy-duty work boots · denim work shirt.

**Subtext (do not state on screen).** His father was an alcoholic who beat him and his mother — which is why he hates alcohol and likes to work. One night his father was beating his sister; Robert punched him in the liver, and shortly after his father's liver failed. The doctors said cirrhosis. He has nightmares and regrets about it. **He fragged three squad leaders in Vietnam.** Nobody saw, nobody knows. He believed it was justified and he was enraged at the time; over the years he has mourned and cried in silence for the men he killed. He doesn't judge Scott for dodging the draft — **he thinks it was a brave thing to do.** Rylee once gave him a stack of pornographic magazines as a joke so he could "loosen up"; she shocked him, but he appreciates her strange way of being, which can sometimes make him smile. **Deep inside, he fears that he really loves violence.**

> **Demo note.** No speaking part. Appears in the Polaroid at the Glade and in Rylee's monologue. Taken by the cult.

## WILLIAM

**William Moore** · Jung archetype: **The Explorer** · Age 27 (b. 1952)
British. Accent: **British, slow, eloquent and elegant.**
6'1" / 185 cm · 79 kg

**Physical.** Dark blond hair, longish and curly. Green eyes. Slim and athletic. **Very popular with the ladies.** Always calm and relaxed, almost taciturn. Clothes reflect his travelling nature — but unlike Scott, **he doesn't travel to run away, he travels to explore.** Freckles, and **a Band-Aid on his nose.**

**Who he is.** An electrical engineer who moved from England to America after graduating, and made a good living opening electronics repair shops. Good at a workbench, but what he really enjoys is travelling: nature, new towns, hiking, discovering a new bar. He moved state to state opening a shop in each, until he settled in Alaska — *"Alaska has the most beautiful landscapes on this earth."* Despite good social skills and a lot of parties, **he lives a rather lonely life and prefers to walk alone.** Met Rylee and Shannon at a hippie party. Clever, thinks fast on his feet. **Sometimes he just vanishes for weeks, but he always returns.** Some people look at his freedom with suspicion.

**Likes:** hiking, technology, candy and sweets.
**Dislikes:** isolation, expectations, big cities.
**Nicknames:** Will · Willy *(Rylee)* · Tinman *(Robert)* · William Wallace *(Tracey, historical banter)*

**Sample lines:**
- *"Sorry darling, I don't think I can quite fix this..."* — responding to Holly's request to fix her totaled truck
- *"You got any jokes on bad jokes?"* — shortly after meeting Rylee
- *"Drifting is bad for you, why don't you just travel?"* — offering Scott a very impersonal opinion

**Inspirations:** Moore's suave **James Bond**; the taciturn **John Lennon**.

**Wardrobe.** Loose brown-orange cargo pants · casual orange shirt with stripes · Oxford shoes · warm gray wool jacket · leather travelling backpack.

**Subtext (do not state on screen).** William is a sex addict, though he has never shown this trait or made advances toward any of his friends. He has one-night stands everywhere he goes, and while he succumbs to his passions he is never satisfied or fulfilled. **The Band-Aid on his nose is from a man who hit him after finding him in bed with his wife**, a few weeks before the game starts. He was sexually abused as a child, lured with candy. Sometimes his daydreaming confuses people; the truth is he is autistic and good at hiding it. He almost always cries when he sleeps alone. **His biggest dream is to marry and have a family.**

> **Demo note.** No speaking part. **His padlocked tent is a gameplay object in Scene 4** — the compass comes from it, and the padlock plus Rylee's "having a peek" story is the only characterisation the demo gives him. Taken by the cult.

## VERNON

**Vernon Shepherd** · Jung archetype: **The Sage** · Age 51 (b. 1928)
American. Accent: American Northwest. **Tired.**
6'2" / 187 cm · 95 kg

**Physical.** A hermit; his personal appearance is rather unkempt. **Unkempt beard, facial hair even on his neck.** Frown lines here and there. A bit overweight, showing a jolly belly. **Faded blue eyes — not electric blue like Holly's.** His clothes, nevertheless, are practical and ready for emergencies. **He gives the impression of a man with no hope left.**

**Who he is.** The lone ranger of Aanniarvik. The post was a gift from the Marine Corps — there is an old military base on the island and they needed someone to watch it. **Vernon was a Marine who served in the Korean War. He was captured and lived as a P.O.W. for a few years under extreme conditions.** Surviving made him a very broken man who now questions the purpose of many things. He is grateful the Corps let him live alone here. A few scheduled times a year he takes the ferry to the mainland to resupply; people who spot him in town avoid him or take him for a lunatic. **Vernon says only what is necessary.** His best friend on the island is his old cat, **Mr. Whiskers**.

**Likes:** Mr. Whiskers, being alone.
**Dislikes:** many things.
**Nicknames:** The boogeyman *(Holly)*

**Sample line:**
- *"May God help us…"* — giving advice to Tracey

**Inspirations:** old **Bruce Wayne** from *The Dark Knight Returns*; old **Laurie Strode** from *Halloween* (2018); **Quint** from *Jaws*.

**Wardrobe.** Feldgrau army trousers · white uniform shirt · feldgrau jungle boots · gray-blue beanie · army suspenders.

**Subtext — MEGA SPOILERS (do not state on screen).** **He is the real father of Holly.** **He is aware of the existence of demons — a fact he discovered in Korea.** He gave up the idea of suicide when he realised his soul was real, and so was hell; that doesn't stop him being tired of this world. **He is a drunkard and a morphine addict.**

> **Why this matters for Scenes 8–10.** He is the only character who knows what is actually happening, and he refuses to explain it — *"we'll talk about that later"*, *"this time the mine is northwest"*, *"you'll die, anything dies"*. His **"God has not abandoned you"** is not a comforting platitude: **he means it literally**, and he is the one person in the story with evidence. His fury at Holly in the cabin is a father's, not an uncle's. He is coughing blood from tuberculosis and shooting morphine, and he still goes out into the dark alone to save Tracey in Scene 10 — **which is where the demo leaves him.**
