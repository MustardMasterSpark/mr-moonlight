## The five traps that already cost time

Every one of these was found the hard way. Read before touching the player prefab.

1. **A `CinemachineCamera` needs a *position* control component.** `Follow` alone does nothing;
   `CinemachinePanTilt` is rotation-only. Use `CinemachineHardLockToTarget` for first person.
   **Fails silently — no error, no warning.**
2. **Every virtual camera on the rig must be driven by `BurntwaxInputBridge`'s `panTilts` array.**
   Driving only one made ADS spin and killed stick look. Add any new vcam to that array.
3. **The camera rig lives under the root but must never go under `Body`.** `Body` is `psm.player`,
   the only transform that yaws; a camera under it feeds its own pan back and the player spins.
4. **Burntwax code that says `transform` usually means the *root*, which now carries our camera,
   arms and HUD.** That is what made crouch squash the whole view. Prefer `player` (= `Body`) for
   anything visual.
5. **Setting `ActiveGunScriptable` in the inspector does not equip a weapon.** Only
   `GunStateMachine.Pickup()` runs the equip path, and it no-ops if the gun is already in
   `CurrentGuns`. Use `BurntwaxStartingLoadout`.

Plus two environment traps:

- **Unity does not tick while the editor is unfocused.** Play-mode checks from a tool session can
  verify *structure* but never motion or feel — `Time.time` freezes while `unscaledTime` advances.
  Anything about feel needs Carlos, and ultimately a build.
- **Prefab Mode + Play Mode raises a modal dialog** (Animation Rigging's `RigBuilder` is
  `[ExecuteInEditMode]`) that **blocks every MCP call** until a human clicks. Answer is
  **Exit Prefab Mode**, never Ignore.
