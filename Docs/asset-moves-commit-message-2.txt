Asset moves: Graveyard Tombstones, The Shed and the Abandoned Swimming Pool (with its assembled prefab) into Mr. Moonlight, import crash countermeasures

DESCRIPTION:
Playground -> Mr. Moonlight asset moves (MRM-88 quick demo update, change trace C-036 on MRM-85). Nothing is placed in a scene.

AST-074 Graveyard Tombstones: all 63 prefabs, 54 FBX, 9 RetroLit materials (BaseColor pixelated at 512, normals 256), vendor GUIDs kept.
AST-108 The Shed (only that prefab): 6 FBX + door controller, 5 RetroLit materials, BaseColor pixelated at 2048 (building, tools atlas) and 1024 (objects) after
the first 1024/512 pass imported clamped at 512 and looked too pixelated; the 11 flat decal quads are deactivated; colliders added (shell and objects LOD0 mesh
colliders, 5 boxes on the tool pieces; door and ivy none; collider policy applied and verified with rays and a capsule through the doorway).
AST-162 Abandoned Swimming Pool (URP version): 148 prefabs, 144 FBX (Read/Write off), 93 RetroLit/URP materials rebuilt from the vendor shader graphs, 89 textures
chosen per material by object size (2048 large structures, 1024 props, 512 only where the source is 512). The scene's whole Meshes object is stored as ONE
prefab, Assembled/AbandonedPool_Assembled.prefab (3,190 nested pieces, 13,380 renderers, 3,148 mesh colliders), built in Playground with PrefabUtility.
Vendor colliders left untouched (collider policy not run on 074 and 162). 3 prefabs with dangling vendor GUIDs were repaired.

- Tools/pipeline: move_pack_to_moonlight_{witch_village,graveyard_tombstones,shed,swimming_pool}.py, preflight_import.py (checks assets BEFORE Unity refreshes),
  assemble_pool_prefab.py (kept as a record; its output froze the editor, do not use it).

canary-tested in Playground first, no preview-scene physics through the bridge, quarantine before restart, verify imported texture sizes.
