MRM-84: Remove colliders from leaf cards on 4 trees (re-run WoodColliderTool) + read-only leaf-card audit tool


DESCRIPTION (the body field)
============================

Carlos found colliders on flat leaf planes when clicking trees in Island_Legion (AP_Tree_04_GTree01_03_SM: a ~35 m2
blob of leaf card 10 m up). Those faces had been painted as wood by mistake. He repainted them out in Technie and the
colliders were rebuilt from the corrected paint.

Audit (new, read-only): Code/Editor/Migration/WoodColliderLeafAudit.cs. Flags painted triangles that sit on an
alpha-cutout submesh where the base-map alpha under the triangle is mostly clear (= visible air). Run() = table of all
109 painted prefabs, Detail(names) = size/height/offset of each suspect group, Highlight(name) = PNG with suspects in
red. Writes only to Temp/TreeColliders/. Found 5 prefabs with leaf-card paint (+2 with ~0 m2 slivers).

Fixed by Carlos in Technie (Painting Data assets): AP_Tree_04_GTree01_03_SM (40 leaf tris), AP_Tree_04_PTree_02_SM (12),
AP_Tree_Blackpoplar01_SM (4), AP_Tree_10_ArgassTree_SM (6). WoodColliderTool.Run re-ran on those 4 plus
AP_Tree_04_M01_04_SM and AP_Tree_04_GTree01_06_SM (paint unchanged, slivers only): 6/6 VERIFY OK, 6/6 RAYTEST OK
(3000 rays each, 0 hits in air, 0 shots through wood). The four now audit at 0 painted leaf tris. Wood mesh assets are
overwritten in place, so every Island_Legion instance picks the change up; Island.unity is untouched.

Left on purpose: AP_Tree_04_M01_05_SM (its 12 flagged tris are thin slivers along branch edges, verified in a
close-up render, not cards), AP_Tree_Deadtree06_SM (586 twig-card tris, Carlos's deliberate choice).

NavMesh: no rebake needed. The removed leaf-card colliders were all 3.5 m or more above ground (agent height 2 m);
the walkable surface cannot have depended on them.
