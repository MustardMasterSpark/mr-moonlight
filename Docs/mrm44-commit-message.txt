MRM-44: Flashlight tuning, hands lit on their own ViewModel light layer, session log v4


DESCRIPTION (the body field)
============================

Second commit on mrm-44 (the first, 777b0ce, added the F-key flashlight). This one tunes the beam, fixes the
first-person hands and weapons being blown out or black under the new lights, and adds the traceability and
docs Carlos asked for.

WHAT'S NEW

- Flashlight beam is now tunable: Flashlight* fields in MoonlightTunables, applied by MoonlightFlashlight, with a
  Live tuning block on the component for tweaking in Play Mode. Carlos's tuned defaults are saved: intensity 60,
  range 40, outer 70, inner 25, colour #FFE094, shadows none, cookie on. New FlashlightUseCookie switch.
- Hands and weapons no longer get blown out. New URP rendering layer "ViewModel" (TagManager). New
  MoonlightViewModelLighting on the player camera puts every mesh under it on that layer only, so the flashlight,
  the personal light and the world sun no longer touch them. The hands get one dedicated directional light
  (ViewModelLight) that follows the LIVE sun every frame, with a night floor so they are never black. Direction,
  colour, day factor and night floor are ViewModel* tunables with live tuning on the Camera.
- Other world lights (Spotter and dropped lamps, flares, fires, muzzle flashes) DO light the hands within 15 m,
  via a 0.5 s scan. The player's own flashlight and personal light never do.
- Guns are PBR again, hands matte: the HQ hand/weapon materials had lost their mask maps in migration and rendered
  as chrome. 21 mask maps restored; arm materials smoothness 0.25. (Assets/ThirdParty is git-ignored: these do not
  appear in this commit.)
- SessionLog v4: [PLAYER] flashlight -> on/off lines and "flashlight on|off handLights N" on every [PERF] line.
- Docs: new Docs/lighting.md (single reference for every light, the day/night system, fog, lamps, tunables and
  traps), new Docs/viewmodel-light-layers.md (deep dive), change record C-001 in Docs/performance-sessions.md
  section 8, CLAUDE.md final-instructions step 4 (change trace) and a lighting.md pointer.

WHAT'S DELIBERATELY NOT HERE

- No time-of-day baselines or dynamic sun: that is the next session (rework of TimeManager, fog and skyboxes).
- No beam sway, visible breath or enemy detection change (still open in MRM-44).
- The hands' light numbers are guesses; Carlos tunes them live and reports the values.
