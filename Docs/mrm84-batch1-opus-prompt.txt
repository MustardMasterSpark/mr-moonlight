===========================================================================
3. TRAPS (learned the hard way)
===========================================================================
- LOOK AT THE IMAGES. The Sonnet sessions called Juniper02 done five times from numbers and one
  far-away screenshot; Carlos found wedges each time. Zoom where limbs meet.
- If Carlos asks a direct question or says stop, answer and wait. Don't keep coding.
- Double paint is normal: in Technie, painting a triangle into a new hull does NOT remove it from
  the hull it was in. The tool gives it to the smaller hull and drops exact duplicates - always
  tell Carlos what it did (PAINT lines) before Apply.
- Report "<-- CHECK" fires on most fluted trunk rings (bark grooves, 0.15-0.3 m) - noise there.
  On a branch or at a fork it's real.
- Prefab Mode Auto Save is on: Apply writes the prefab immediately (Undo works; Restore undoes).
- execute_code often returns an empty result right after a recompile or asset refresh - retry,
  and after anything destructive, re-read the real state instead of assuming.
- Asset deletes via execute_code need safety_checks=false. TreeColliderReset's keep list must hold
  every Done prefab.
- Colliders live on the `Visual` child, not the prefab root.
