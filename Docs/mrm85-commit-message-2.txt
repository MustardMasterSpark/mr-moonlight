MRM-85: Culling C in every forest scene, corpse optimization, corpse dissolve and removal (RetroLit burn dissolve)

DESCRIPTION:
Performance work on mrm-86 (Linear MRM-85). Change records C-012 and C-013 in
Docs/performance-sessions.md section 8; details in Docs/corpse-optimization.md.

Culling C locked (C-012): AST-145 Dynamic Culling, FullDisable, now in Island (5,990
trees), Island_Legion and LightingTestScene (8,786 each): controller, camera on
PlayerCamera, baked layer-29 colliders on every Gaia tree. Sandbox, MainMenu and the
galleries have no Gaia trees. (The AST-145 asset itself stays git-ignored.)

Corpse optimization (C-012): new CorpseOptimizer settle pass 5 s after the AI strip:
shadows off, Animator off, GoreSimulator + blood particles off, ragdoll joints and
rigidbodies destroyed, dropped shotgun shows LOD1 only, corpse culling through AST-145
(CorpseCullingHooks; the bridge lives in the git-ignored asset folder). EnemyDeathDrop
exposes DroppedItems. 10 Corpse* tunables in MoonlightTunables. Legion mode only: story
mode must turn CorpseDisableGore, CorpseStripPhysics and CorpseDissolveEnabled off.

Corpse dissolve (C-013): the AST-063 Dissolve FX "Burn Dissolve" look ported into RetroLit
as an opt-in _USE_DISSOLVE feature (RetroLit.shader, RetroSurfaceInput.hlsl, depth passes;
off for every existing material), noise texture Art/VFX/Dissolve/Dissolve_Noise.png, five
*_Dissolve.mat variants, CorpseDissolve component on Enemy_Spotter.prefab, editor menu
Mr. Moonlight > Corpse Dissolve > Rebuild Material Variants. After the settle pass: 3 s,
body burns 3 s, lamp and props burn 3 s while the lamp light dims (LampFireEffect
.FadeOutAndFinish), then the enemy, its drops, its culling proxy and registrations are
destroyed. This replaces the earlier "lamp keeps its own timeline" rule for corpses.

SessionLog v5.2 / v5.3: enemy counts read "corpses N (settled S, dissolved D, culling
C/R)", corpses is the live count, two [CORPSE] lines per dissolved corpse.

Build 43 analysis (Docs/performance-sessions.md section 5, new H13): corpse geometry cost
fell 40% at 30+ corpses (draws -44%, SetPass -39%, +21% fps at 30-59 corpses) but the frame
at 60-100 corpses did not improve: dynamic lights (alive and dropped lamps, fires) are the
next suspect.
