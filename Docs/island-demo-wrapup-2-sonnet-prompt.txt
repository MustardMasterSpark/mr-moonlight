**2) Shotgun death-drop fixed — took three real bugs to actually fix, read this before touching
EnemyDeathDrop.cs again**
Carlos's ask: the dropped shotgun should fall to the ground and stay exactly where it lands
(previously it went flying/rolling far away). Chased through three distinct root causes, in
order:
  a. **First attempt (raycast snap) was wrong**: tried to just raycast down and teleport the item
     to the hit point. Failed visibly — left the shotgun floating in mid-air when the raycast
     missed (screenshot evidence). Replaced with real physics: falls under gravity with a real
     Rigidbody, then a coroutine (`FreezeWhenSettled`) waits for `Rigidbody.IsSleeping()` and only
     then disables the collider and destroys the Rigidbody. This part of the design is right and
     stayed.
  b. **Real cause #1 — interpenetration explosion**: the new collider was spawning inside the
     dying enemy's own CapsuleCollider, and PhysX's depenetration response launched it sideways
     before gravity ever got a say. Fixed by adding the (pre-existing, unused) "DroppedProp"
     project layer (9) and setting the physics collision matrix — a PERSISTENT project setting in
     ProjectSettings/DynamicsManager.asset, not a per-instance script fix — to ignore DroppedProp
     vs Enemy(6), vs Ragdoll(26), and vs Health(10). All death-drops (`EnemyDeathDrop.cs`) now get
     reassigned to this layer via `AssignDroppedPropLayer`, recursively (multi-part meshes).
  c. **Real cause #2, the actual one — sphere colliders don't stop rolling**: even after (b), the
     drop still traveled 30-45m. `EnsureCollider`'s fallback is a SphereCollider, and a sphere has
     essentially zero rolling resistance — on this island's slopes/cliffs it just rolls downhill
     forever under gravity alone, no impulse needed at all. This is almost certainly what caused
     Carlos's ORIGINAL bug report too, not the (modest) scatter impulse he suspected. Fixed by
     setting `body.constraints = RigidbodyConstraints.FreezeRotation` on the shotgun's rigidbody
     (ground-snap path only — the lamp already has its own BoxCollider, no sphere, not affected,
     Carlos never complained about it, left alone). Verified live three times after this fix:
     lands within centimetres of the death point every time, collider disabled, rigidbody gone.
  **Lesson for next time a "physics prop launches across the map" bug shows up on this project:
  check the collider shape before anything else. A sphere fallback on sloped terrain will look
  exactly like an impulse bug and isn't one.**
- Found incidentally, NOT fixed (flag if it comes up again): the Spotter has ~33 always-active
  `Hitbox_*` colliders on layer "Health" wrapping the whole body for damage detection, and they
  are solid (`isTrigger: false`), not triggers. That's a live physics collision hazard for any
  prop near an enemy, not just death drops. Out of scope this session, just flagged.
- Also found incidentally: killing a Spotter via a direct `EnemyHealth.Kill()` call can hit a
  `NullReferenceException` inside `GoreModuleCut.ExecuteCut` (Pampel Games Gore Simulator) on a
  random dismemberment roll. Didn't chase it — not something today's changes touched — but it's
  a real crash path, worth a look if dismemberment ever looks broken in a playtest.

**3) Corpses now go inert (new `EnemyCorpseCleanup`)**
- New component, wired as a 4th persistent listener on `EnemyHealth.Died` (alongside the existing
  EnemyDeathDrop.DropAll / EnemyRangedAttack.CancelBurst / EnemyAudioHooks.PlayDeath calls) on the
  Enemy_Spotter prefab.
- After `Tunables.EnemyCorpseCleanupDelay` (3.5s — deliberately delayed, see below), disables
  every collider in the corpse (player walks through it) and every AI/attack/patrol behaviour:
  BlazeAI, every `BlazeAISpace.BlazeBehaviour`-derived state script, BlazeAISpareState,
  NavMeshAgent, EnemyRangedAttack, EnemyFirearm, EnemyReinforcementSpawner, SpotterFlareCall,
  SpotterPanicCall, EnemyPatrolRoute, EnemyAudioHooks, EnemyDebugControls.
- Leaves Animator, AudioSource, GoreSimulator, EnemyIdentity, EnemyHealth, EnemyDeathDrop alone.
  The GameObject itself is never destroyed or deactivated — Carlos's explicit ask was a corpse
  that stays, just inert.
- **Why the delay**: `Died` fires BEFORE Blaze's own death handling (ragdoll settle, keyframed
  death animation) runs later in the same `Kill()` call. Disabling BlazeAI/NavMeshAgent
  immediately would cut that sequence off mid-play. 3.5s is a guess at "long enough" — if a
  playtest shows the death animation still glitching, or the corpse staying solid/blocking for
  too long before you can walk through it, this is the tunable to adjust
  (`MoonlightTunables.EnemyCorpseCleanupDelay`).
- Verified live: `EnemyRangedAttack` etc. still enabled immediately after death (proves the delay
  is real), all disabled and all 32 colliders off ~5s later, zero console errors, Animator still
  enabled throughout.
