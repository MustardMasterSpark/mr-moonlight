SUMMARY:
MRM-85: Unlock fps (vSync off at boot), AST-145 Dynamic Culling test scenes, SessionLog v5.1, forest test start

DESCRIPTION:
Performance work on mrm-86 (Linear MRM-85, touches MRM-78's vSync area).

FPS unlock (C-010): Polymind GraphicsOptions.Apply() forces vSyncCount = 1 at every
boot and our override lived only in MainMenu's SettingsPanel, so every other start
scene ran at the monitor's 75 Hz. New DisplayBootSettings (BeforeSceneLoad) applies
GameSettings.VSyncEnabled (default off) and targetFrameRate = -1 in any scene.

SessionLog v5.1: weather profile in every [PERF] window, [WORLD] weather / tree fires
lines (keyed on profile names, not the blend value), vsync + cap in [PERF].

AST-145 Advanced Culling System 2, Dynamic module (C-011), tested in separate scenes:
LightingTestSceneCullingTest (KeepShadows) and LightingTestSceneCullingTestFull
(FullDisable) = copies of LightingTestScene with DC_Controller, DC_Camera on PlayerCamera
and baked DC_Collider + DC_SourceSettings on 8,786 trees. New layer ACSCulling (29):
TagManager, DynamicsManager, Physics2DSettings. The asset itself is in git-ignored
Assets/ThirdParty/AST-145. Rollback: Docs/culling-rollback.ps1.

LightingTestScene: player spawn and weather circuit moved to a dense-forest test spot
(player 218.06, 33.37, -5.23, yaw 311.3; was 403.60, 23.10, -74.20).

Results (builds 37-42, Docs/culling-ast145.md section 10): culling +13-19% fps at the
spawn, +31-35% at the Fountain, draws -66% to -87%; FullDisable beats KeepShadows by
3-5%. Corpses dominate the fight (9.7 ms -> 24-29 ms at 60-100 corpses, SetPass ~600 ->
~2,000) with or without culling. Fires cost 2.2-3.1 ms. H11/H12 added, H1 strengthened.
