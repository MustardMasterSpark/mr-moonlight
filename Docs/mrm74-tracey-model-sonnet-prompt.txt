Recorded here only so nobody re-proposes it: the OLD description said the plan was to "retarget
the HQ FPS Weapons 2.0 animation set onto her rig" via Retarget Pro. **That is impossible**, proven
on 2026-09-05 by reading the actual data, not by opinion:

  - The weapon clips live on `Arms_Root`: 44 bones, TWO root bones (UpperArm.L / UpperArm.R), and
    NO hips, spine, chest or clavicle. Unity's Humanoid avatar requires a hips->spine->chest->
    shoulder chain, so this skeleton can never be a Humanoid avatar at all. The vendor ships it
    Generic (`animationType: 2`) for exactly this reason.
  - The clips are authored in CAMERA space — the arm roots are ParentConstraint-ed onto
    `IMotionMixer.TargetTransform`. A reload clip means "put the hand here relative to the lens",
    never "rotate the shoulder by X". There is no shoulder rotation in the data to retarget.
  - Humanoid retargeting is rotation-based and deliberately discards exact positions, which would
    break weapon-in-hand alignment even if the first two problems vanished.

The correct architecture — TWO skeletons that never merge — is now stated in the issue itself, and
in `Docs/tracey-rig-strategy.md`. Issue and docs agree. Nothing is pending.

=====================================================================
THE THREE TRAPS ALREADY PAID FOR — DO NOT REDISCOVER THESE
=====================================================================
Full detail in Docs/tracey-rig-strategy.md §9 and the guide's Appendix B.

1. BLENDER'S DEFAULT FBX EXPORT IS 100x TOO SMALL.
   Fix in Blender: `apply_scale_options='FBX_SCALE_ALL'` with `global_scale=1.0`. NOT with a Scale
   Factor of 100 in the ModelImporter — that hides it and breaks the next re-export.
   ⚠ This is the OPPOSITE direction to the AccuRig scale bug (which needs 0.01). Do not reason
   about which multiplier applies. COMPARE MESH BOUNDS AGAINST A KNOWN-GOOD VENDOR MESH and let
   the numbers decide. Both scale bugs are real and they point opposite ways.

2. BLENDER RECALCULATES BONE ROLL AND SILENTLY TWISTS FINGERS.
   Round-tripping a rigged vendor FBX through Blender returned 15 of 44 bones with bindpose
   rotations off by up to 0.109 — every left-hand finger plus the whole right forearm chain.
   Binding that to the vendor skeleton twists exactly those joints, and it only shows up
   mid-reload, on one hand, reading as "the animation is broken."
   Bone ORDER survives, so the fix is to transplant the vendor mesh's bindpose array wholesale.
   `MoonlightTraceyArmsBuild` already does this on every run and aborts if the skeleton stops
   matching. Do not remove that step.

3. THE VIEWMODEL ARMS ARE NOT RETROLIT, AND CANNOT BE.
   They use `Shader Graphs/LitFieldOfView_SSS`. The FOV nodes in that graph are what pull the arms
   to their own 60 degree FOV (`CameraFOVHandler`, global `_FOV` / `_FOVEnabled`). RetroLit has no
   such node — RetroLit arms would render at world FOV and CLIP THROUGH WALLS.
   MRM-9's "hands are RetroLit" ruling therefore cannot hold literally. OPEN QUESTION for Carlos:
   if he wants the retro finish on hands, the FOV nodes must be ported into a RetroLit viewmodel
   variant first. That is very likely the same unfinished `RetroLitViewModel.shader` already
   blamed in the `mrm9_weapon_darkness_deferred` memory — worth solving once for weapons and hands
   together, as its own job. Do not bodge it per-material.


=====================================================================
THE ACCURIG FACT THAT MAKES ALL OF THIS CHEAP
=====================================================================
AccuRig's arm maps 1:1 by NAME onto the vendor arms rig — 19 of 22 bones per arm:

  CC_Base_L_Upperarm        -> UpperArm.L
  CC_Base_L_Forearm         -> Forearm.L
  CC_Base_L_Hand            -> Hand.L
  CC_Base_L_Index1/2/3      -> Index.1.L  / Index.2.L  / Index.3.L
  CC_Base_L_Mid1/2/3        -> Middle.1.L / Middle.2.L / Middle.3.L
  CC_Base_L_Ring1/2/3       -> Ring.1.L   / Ring.2.L   / Ring.3.L
  CC_Base_L_Pinky1/2/3      -> Pinky.1.L  / Pinky.2.L  / Pinky.3.L
  CC_Base_L_Thumb1/2/3      -> Thumb.1.L  / Thumb.2.L  / Thumb.3.L
  (no counterpart)          -> ForearmTwist.1-4.L   [fold into Forearm]

So moving weights between an AccuRig arm and the vendor arm is a DICTIONARY LOOKUP, not a spatial
transfer. AccuRig stays the first-pass rigger exactly as it was for the Spotter. No "Frankenstein"
merged rig is needed, and Carlos has been told this.

Vendor rest pose, for reference when pose-matching (real human scale, relaxed A-pose):
  UpperArm.L head=(0.212, 0.044, 1.375) tail=(0.319, 0.046, 1.213)
  Forearm.L  head=(0.319, 0.046, 1.213) tail=(0.402, 0.017, 1.113)
  Hand.L     head=(0.484, -0.011, 1.014)
⚠ DO NOT change the vendor bone LENGTHS. The animations are authored for those proportions;
stretching a bone throws the weapon out of the hand. Fit the mesh to the bones, not the reverse.
